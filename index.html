What do these numbers mean?

A lock-in is when you finish the questions after a timer, a nudge or a daily check-in. Each one is a moment you chose your goal over scrolling.

🔥 Day streak counts the days in a row you finished at least one lock-in. It builds the habit of checking in with your goal every day. Miss a day and it starts over.

✅ Today shows how many times you caught yourself and refocused today.

⚡ All time is every lock-in you have ever finished, a running total of the effort you put toward your goal.

How to block your apps too

A web page can't lock other apps. Use your phone's built-in limits and let this app stop you after.

iPhone: Settings → Screen Time → App Limits.

Android: Settings → Digital Wellbeing → App timers.

Add this page to your Home Screen from the Share menu. Keep it open in the background so alerts fire.

🔳 Turn on grayscale

A black and white screen makes apps less tempting to open.

iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Color Filters → turn on Color Filters → choose Grayscale. Tip: in Settings → Accessibility → Accessibility Shortcut, pick Color Filters. Then triple-click the side button to switch grayscale on and off.

Android: Settings → Digital Wellbeing and parental controls → Bedtime mode → Grayscale. Menu names can differ a little on each phone.
